# DuitNow P2P: making a direct poster-to-helper payment feel in-app

Date: 2026-10-05. Research only, not legal advice. Context: `2026-10-05-stripe-feasibility.md`, `2026-10-05-wise-feasibility.md`, `2026-10-05-payments.md`.

- **[I]** means my own inference from the quoted text.
- PayNet's consumer site (`paynet.my`) and developer docs (`docs.paynet.my`) render client-side. I read the text from their own data files: `https://paynet.my/resources/faq.json` and the page payload of each `docs.paynet.my` page. The quotes are verbatim from those.

Model under study: Tapiro never touches money. After a task, the poster pays the helper directly from the poster's own bank or e-wallet app.

## 1. DuitNow QR for individuals

### Personal static QR: yes

- PayNet: "Scan and pay, send money or share your QR to receive funds." https://paynet.my/personal-solutions/duitnow-qr.html
- PayNet transfer steps: "Scan the recipient’s QR code and key in the amount." Same URL.
- RHB: "Select 'Receive'. Then, the DuitNow QR code will be generated that's linked to your RHB Current/Savings Account." https://www.rhbgroup.com/others/faq/payments-and-transfer/duitnow-qr/index.html
- RHB: "Can I save and share the DuitNow QR code that I generated? Yes, you can by selecting 'Share QR' then save or share the QR image with others to receive money." Same URL.
- Hong Leong: "Show your QR Code to the payer to scan and make payment to you". https://www.hlb.com.my/en/personal-banking/hong-leong-connect/qr-pay.html
- CIMB lists "How do I generate or share my DuitNow QR code?" under "Transfer funds with QR". https://www.cimb.com.my/en/personal/digital-services/duitnowqr.html
- TNG: "P2P seller is an existing Touch ‘n Go eWallet user that is currently using their personal QR to receive payments for their goods or services sold to customers." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17608981927577-What-is-P2P-seller
- TNG lists "DuitNow P2P QR" as a way to receive. https://www.tngdigital.com.my/get-started/transfer/duitnow/

### Amount and reference in a P2P QR: the standard allows it; banks limit it

- The national QR spec is EMV-based: "The DuitNow National QR Code Standard is referenced to the following publications: EMV® QR Code Specification for Payment Systems (EMV QRCPS) Merchant-Presented Mode". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/qr-generation-specification/merchant-presented-mode
- Data objects (https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/qr-data-object):
  - Point of initiation `01`: "11 – Static QR Code 12 – Dynamic QR Code".
  - Merchant Category Code `52`: "“0000” to be used for P2P QRs".
  - Transaction Amount `54`: conditional. "The amount shall not be included if the mobile application should prompt the consumer to enter the value".
  - Additional Data `62` → Reference Label `05`: "Any value as defined by merchant or Acquirer in order to identify the transaction." Purpose of Transaction `08` also exists.
- PayNet's own sample is a P2P QR with an amount: "Example 2: Merchant-Presented Mode QR on Payment P2P, without Data Integrity Check", MCC "0000 – User P2P", "Amount – 10.00". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/examples
- Bank apps that generate an amount QR make it short-lived:
  - RHB: "the QR code with requested amount only valid for 60 seconds." (RHB URL above)
  - Hong Leong: "You can only share a static QR Code i.e. the one without a pre-set amount. A Dynamic QR code, where you have set the amount to receive, cannot be shared out and has a 60 seconds validity". (HLB URL above)
- [I] A helper can show an amount QR on their own phone face to face. A shareable QR image is static, so the poster types the amount.

### Can a third-party app generate a valid DuitNow QR from a DuitNow ID: no

- The QR does not carry the DuitNow ID. It carries an acquirer-assigned ID. Merchant Account Information `26`:
  - Acquirer ID `01`: mandatory, from PayNet's participant table (banks and e-money issuers).
  - QR ID `02`: "Merchant/Recipient Identification Number assigned by Acquirer."
  - Mobile Number `04`: optional, "Merchant mobile number."
  - Source: qr-data-object URL above.
- [I] Tapiro cannot turn a phone number or NRIC into a payable QR. Only the helper's bank or e-wallet can issue the QR ID.
- Re-encoding the helper's own QR is technically possible. Parse the helper's static QR, add tag `54` (amount) and `62/05` (reference), set `01` to `12`, recompute CRC `63`. The CRC is a public algorithm: "[ISO/IEC 13239] using the polynomial '1021' (hex) and initial value 'FFFF'". Same URL.
  - Whether payer apps accept a QR whose QR ID was issued for a static code but now carries an amount: **[U]**.
  - Optional Data Integrity Check `82` ("To carry hash value or any other authentication value") could make some issuers reject altered codes: **[U]**.
  - Whether PayNet rules allow a non-participant to alter or generate DuitNow QR codes: **[U]**. The QR rulebook is not public. [I] Treat it as not allowed until PayNet says yes.
- Joining the network needs a licence. PayNet: "Non-Banks seeking admission to RPP shall be: An Approved Issuer of a Designated Payment Instrument, A Registered Merchant Acquirer, An Authorised Financial Technology Provider, An Approved Statutory Body". https://paynet.my/resources/faq.json (DuitNow → Transfer → "How can fintechs and payment providers participate in DuitNow Transfer?")
- Ecosystem access also needs "Appropriate licenses, approvals or regulatory status" and "paid-up capital or net shareholder's equity requirements". https://paynet.my/financial-ecosystem/ecosystem-access.html

## 3. Deep links into bank and e-wallet apps

- No bank or e-wallet page I could read publishes a URL scheme or universal link for a prefilled DuitNow transfer. **[U]** for all nine apps. Undocumented schemes can change without notice. [I]
- **DuitNow Online Banking/Wallets (the app-to-app redirect) is merchant-only.**
  - "From the merchant portal or mobile app, customers select their preferred payment method, are redirected to their bank or wallet to approve the transaction, and then return to the merchant site". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/online-banking-wallet/overview
  - Roles: "A merchant acquirer is the entity that enables merchants to accept DuitNow Online Banking/Wallet (DOBW) payments." Same URL.
- **DuitNow Pay is merchant-only and goes through an acquirer.**
  - "To be a part of the DuitNow Pay platform, you will need to be affiliated with our merchant acquirers' network." https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-pay/registration/introduction
  - Consumer FAQ: "DuitNow Pay is available at participating online retailers and businesses in Malaysia." https://paynet.my/resources/faq.json
- [I] The money would land in a merchant account, which is Tapiro or its PSP. That is collection, which the "never touch money" rule forbids. A non-merchant app cannot use DOBW or DuitNow Pay to send a poster into a P2P transfer to a helper.

## 4. DuitNow Transfer by phone number or account

- What the payer needs: only the DuitNow ID. "Link your DuitNow ID, such as your mobile number, MyKad, passport or army or police number". "Once linked, payers can send money instantly with no bank account number needed." https://paynet.my/personal-solutions/duitnow-transfer.html and faq.json.
- Foreign students can receive. CIMB: "Yes, you may register your mobile number or passport number as DuitNow ID." https://www.cimb.com.my/en/personal/help-support/faq/cimb-clicks-online-banking/accounts-and-transactions/transfer-funds/duitnow-to-mobile-id/i-am-a-foreigner-can-i-register-for-duitnow.html
- One ID maps to one account: "Each ID can only be linked to one bank account." faq.json.
- **Name enquiry exists and is part of the flow.**
  - Network: Pay by Proxy "requires initiating a Proxy Resolution Enquiry request ... to retrieve the recipient’s name for confirmation. After the customer confirms the transaction details, including the Proxy ID and beneficiary name, the participant sends a Credit Request". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/transfer/integration/pay-by-proxy
  - Recommended confirmation fields include "Beneficiary account name", "Payment amount", "Recipient reference". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/validations
  - CIMB: "once you have entered the DuitNow ID, the recipient’s name associated with the DuitNow ID will be displayed, please verify recipient’s name prior to confirm transaction." https://www.cimb.com.my/en/personal/help-support/faq/cimb-clicks-online-banking/accounts-and-transactions/transfer-funds/duitnow-to-mobile-id/how-can-i-ensure-that-the-person-i-am-transferring-funds-to-is-the-correct-person.html
  - RHB: "verify the recipient's name before confirming the transaction." https://www.rhbgroup.com/others/faq/payments-and-transfer/duitnow/index.html
- [I] Tapiro can show the helper's verified display name next to their DuitNow ID. The bank then shows the registered name. If the two match, a mistyped or swapped ID is caught before money moves.
- Fees and limits: "absolutely FREE for consumers to send and receive money up to RM 5,000". "Consumers may transfer up to RM 50,000 per transaction at banks." faq.json.

### DuitNow Request: the closest thing to "approve a payment"

- "All you need is the payer’s DuitNow ID such as their mobile number, MyKad, passport or army or police number. Once they approve your request, the funds go straight into your Current or Savings account, Line of Credit (LOC) or eWallet." https://paynet.my/personal-solutions/duitnow-request.html
- Payer side: "Review the payment details, approve it and you are done. The requester receives a real-time notification once it is successful." Same URL.
- RHB: requests expire "after 7 days"; payer can "Pay, Reject or Forward". https://www.rhbgroup.com/others/faq/payments-and-transfer/duitnow-request/index.html
- The network spec frames Request as a merchant/biller product ("DuitNow Request allows Merchants/Billers to send a payment request to Customers"). https://docs.paynet.my/docs/request/overview
  - Bank apps offer it to individuals anyway (RHB above). Which of the nine apps support sending or receiving a Request: **[U]** except RHB.
- [I] The helper sends a Request from their own bank app to the poster's phone number. The poster gets an in-bank-app prompt with the amount and only taps approve. Cost: the poster must reveal a phone number or ID to the helper.

## 5. Alipay and WeChat between two people in Malaysia

- **No official P2P transfer deep link found.** Alipay and WeChat document app-to-app launch only for merchant payment SDKs. **[U]** for any public `alipays://` or `weixin://` transfer scheme.
- Alipay "APP支付" pays the signing merchant: "APP支付是指商家在商家移动端 App 中集成支付宝 SDK，调起支付宝来完成付款的一种支付产品。" Supported account types: "支付宝企业账号、支付宝个人账号、个体工商户". Applications need an app whose content matches "营业执照经营范围". https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/00dn73.md
- WeChat Pay "APP支付" is also merchant collection: "APP支付，提供商户在自己的APP中使用微信支付收款的能力。" Entity types: "个体工商户、企业、事业单位、政府机关、社会组织". https://pay.weixin.qq.com/doc/v3/merchant/4013070158
- [I] Both put money into Tapiro's merchant account. Both conflict with the "never touch money" rule. A Malaysian company may also not qualify as a mainland merchant type **[U]**.
- Personal collection codes:
  - Alipay's own doc index says its 当面付 changelog moved "收款码能力" to "经营码". https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/llms.txt (entry for https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/074mkv.md) [I] Personal codes used for selling services are being pushed into a business product.
  - The PRC central bank rules on personal codes used for business (often cited as 银发〔2021〕259号): **[U]**, not fetched.
  - Wallet acceptable-use rules forbid "Academic fraud, essay ghostwriting, proxy test taking" (WeChat Pay) and similar for Alipay; see `2026-10-05-payments.md` §4.
- Cross-border: both parties hold CNY wallets tied to mainland identity. A Malaysian service paid inside China's system raises PRC foreign-exchange and Malaysian exchange-control questions: **[U]**.
- Legitimate MYR→Weixin path exists: TNG offers "Transfer directly from TNG eWallet to a WeChat (Weixin) account" for "A flat RM21 service fee". https://www.tngdigital.com.my/promotions/transfer-directly-to-weixin-wechat/ [I] Too costly for small bounties, and it is a remittance to China, not local P2P.
- [I] Tapiro should not route posters to Alipay or WeChat personal codes. Students already have Malaysian bank accounts or TNG; DuitNow covers them.

## 6. iOS capabilities

- **Open another app.** `open(_:options:completionHandler:)`: "If the specified URL scheme is handled by another app, iOS launches that app". It "isn’t constrained by the LSApplicationQueriesSchemes requirement". https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiapplication/open(_:options:completionhandler:) and canOpenURL page below.
- **Check if an app is installed.** `canOpenURL(_:)`: "you must declare the URL schemes you pass to this method by adding the LSApplicationQueriesSchemes key". "Apps linked on or after iOS 15 are limited to a maximum of 50 entries ... Apps linked on or after iOS 27 are limited to a maximum of 25 entries". "This method always returns false for undeclared schemes". https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiapplication/canopenurl(_:)
  - Expo: `Linking.canOpenURL` rejects "on iOS if you didn't add the specific scheme in the LSApplicationQueriesSchemes key inside Info.plist". https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/linking/
  - [I] Without published bank schemes (§3), this only helps if Tapiro uses observed, undocumented schemes. Nine apps fit under 25.
- **Universal links.** "if no app is available to handle a universal link, iOS routes it to the person’s default browser". Same canOpenURL URL.
- **Save a QR image to Photos.** Add-only access uses `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`: "This key is required if your app uses APIs that have write access to the user’s photo library." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsphotolibraryaddusagedescription
  - "If your app only adds to the library, use the NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription key." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/delivering-an-enhanced-privacy-experience-in-your-photos-app
  - Expo SDK 57: `MediaLibrary.saveToLibraryAsync` is "Deprecated: Use Asset.create() or import this method from expo-media-library/legacy." https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/media-library/
- **Share sheet.** `UIActivityViewController` offers "copying items to the pasteboard, posting content to social media sites, sending items via email or SMS, and more. Apps can also define custom services." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiactivityviewcontroller
  - Whether any Malaysian bank app has a share extension that accepts a QR image: **[U]**. [I] Assume not.
- **Copy to clipboard.** Always possible. [I] It is the most reliable hand-off: copy ID, copy amount, copy reference.

## 7. Verifying that a payment happened

- **No official verification for a non-participant.** Status APIs are network messages between participants. "When there's a timeout at RPP ... the status of the transaction becomes unknown." Handled by Transaction Enquiry. https://docs.paynet.my/docs/transfer/overview [I] Only banks and licensed participants can call it.
- Network transaction ID format (Business Message Identifier): "Format: YYYYMMDDBBBBBBBBXXXOCCSSSSSSSS", where "YYYYMMDD = Date", "BBBBBBBB = BIC Code", "XXX = Transaction Type", "O = Originator", "CC = Channel", "SSSSSSSS = Serial Number". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/transfer/mapping-tables
  - Whether consumer receipts show this ID, and which field: **[U]**. [I] A format check could flag obvious fakes. It cannot prove a payment.
- Evidence the payer has:
  - Shareable e-receipts. CIMB OCTO: "Forgot to share receipt? Fret not! Simply share it with the recipient whenever you need it." https://www.cimb.com.my/en/personal/digital-services/cimb-octo-app.html
  - Transaction history. CIMB: "log into CIMB Clicks to verify the recipient’s details". RHB: "verify the recipient details in Transaction History."
- Evidence the helper has, which is stronger:
  - Incoming notification. PayNet: "Yes, you will get notifications for incoming payments.*" (*subject to provider T&C). faq.json.
  - DuitNow Request: "The requester receives a real-time notification once it is successful." Request page above.
- [I] Screenshots are easy to fake. The helper's "Received" tap should be the source of truth. The payer's screenshot is supporting evidence for disputes only.
- [I] Put a short Tapiro task code in the transfer's recipient reference. The helper can then match the incoming credit to the task.

## 8. Legal: MSBA as amended by Act A1711

- In force: "Act A1711, that was gazetted on 6 June 2024 and came into force on 1 August 2024." https://www.bnm.gov.my/legislation
- Text: https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/820862/act-A1711-msba2024-en.pdf
- New definition, s.4(b) of A1711: "“facilitating the transfer of funds” includes any one or more of the following acts: (a) offering services to transfer funds; (b) accepting or receiving funds; (c) transporting funds; (d) arranging for transfer of funds; (e) issuing receipt for transfer of funds; (f) utilising a system to transfer funds; (g) allowing an account to be used for transfer or receipt of funds; or (h) engaging in any form of settlement activity, including net settlement, set-off and debt assignment, arising from transfer of funds".
- New "remittance business", s.4(g): "(a) the business of transferring funds; or (b) the business of facilitating transfer of funds, whether in any form or by any means or whether there is any movement of funds or not, on behalf of an originator person in or outside Malaysia to a beneficiary person in or outside Malaysia ... but excludes such other business, activity, system or arrangement as the Bank may prescribe".
- Penalty, amended s.4(4): "imprisonment for a term not exceeding ten years and a fine of not less than fifty thousand ringgit but not exceeding five million ringgit."
- Abetment, new s.86(3), illustration (b): "A, a remittance system provider, who knows B is not a licensee under this Act, continues to provide B with a system or application that supports B’s remittance business activity. A abets the commission of an offence".

Assessment:

- **[I]** Showing a helper's own DuitNow ID or their bank-issued static QR, with no Tapiro fee on the transfer, is closer to a marketplace showing a seller's contact details than to "arranging for transfer of funds". Tapiro does not act "on behalf of an originator person". No funds or instructions pass through it. It earns nothing per transfer.
- **[I]** The words are wide: "in any form or by any means or whether there is any movement of funds or not". The risk rises if Tapiro:
  - builds or alters the payment instruction (generating or re-encoding QR codes with amount and reference);
  - charges a fee tied to the transfer, or nets fees against it (limb (h), "set-off");
  - calls its own record a "receipt" (limb (e), "issuing receipt for transfer of funds").
- **[U]** Whether this reading holds. No BNM guidance or prescribed exclusion for P2P marketplaces was found. A Malaysian lawyer should confirm before launch. Add it to the lawyer list in `2026-10-05-payments.md` §7.
- Out of scope but still blocking: whether a student-pass holder may be paid for tasks at all (`2026-10-05-payments.md` §5).

## Recommended "most in-app-like" flow

Built only on confirmed capabilities. Tapiro never builds, alters or carries a payment instruction.

1. **Helper setup (once).** The helper adds a DuitNow ID: their phone number or passport number. They may also upload their bank's static DuitNow QR image. Tapiro stores both as they are and does not re-encode the QR. Tapiro shows the helper's registered name as typed by the helper, with a note that the bank will show the real one.
2. **Task done → "Pay [helper]" sheet** on the poster's phone. It is a native sheet and Tapiro's only payment UI:
   - helper's name and avatar;
   - amount, large, with a **Copy** button;
   - DuitNow ID, with a **Copy** button;
   - reference, for example `TPR-7K2Q`, with a **Copy** button and a hint to paste it into "Recipient reference";
   - a line: "Check that your bank shows **[name]** before you confirm."
   - Wording rules: no "receipt", no "Tapiro Pay", no "secure payment". Use "Pay directly in your bank app".
3. **Open the bank app.** A row of the poster's bank and e-wallet apps. The poster picks one once, and Tapiro remembers it. Tapiro opens the app's home screen only, never a transfer screen. Ship this only after on-device testing, because no scheme is published (§3). Fallback: "Open your bank app and choose DuitNow".
4. **Optional QR path.** If the helper uploaded a QR, show it full-screen with **Save to Photos** for "pay from another phone or from gallery". Label gallery import as unconfirmed in internal notes. Hide this path when the poster's chosen app is TNG and the QR is bank-issued.
5. **Optional Request path.** The helper's "Request payment" tells the helper to send a DuitNow Request from their own bank app to the poster's DuitNow ID. This only works if the poster agreed to share it. The poster approves inside the bank app. It feels most like in-app payment, but costs privacy, so make it opt-in.
6. **Confirm.** The poster taps "I've paid" and may attach a receipt screenshot. The helper taps "Received" after seeing the bank notification and the reference. Only the helper's tap closes the task's payment state. Mismatches go to the existing report flow.

Effort (rough): steps 1, 2 and 6 are pure app UI. Step 3 needs a short device test per bank app. Steps 4 and 5 are optional.
