# Payee setup: how a helper adds receiving methods

Date: 2026-10-06. Research only, not legal advice. Builds on `2026-10-05-duitnow-p2p.md` (cited below as "P2P report").

Conventions:

- Every fact has a quote and a URL fetched in this session.
- **[U]** means I could not confirm it from a primary source.
- **[I]** means my own inference from the quoted text.
- PayNet developer pages render client-side. I read the text from each page's `__NUXT_DATA__` payload. Apple pages were read from their documentation JSON (`developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/...`).
- Not reachable or not found this session: the People's Bank of China (PBC) notices on personal collection codes, Alipay and WeChat consumer help pages, TNG terms and conditions (URL returns "Page not found"), EMVCo specification PDFs (download is gated), Maybank and Public Bank. Everything that depends on those is **[U]**.

Model: Tapiro never touches money. The helper shows how to pay them. The poster pays from their own app.

## 1. Expiry and validity

### DuitNow QR from a bank (static, no amount)

- Banks call the shareable code static and do not state an expiry.
  - HLB: "Show your QR Code to the payer to scan for immediate payment. Payer will have to manually enter the payment amount for this Static QR Code." https://www.hlb.com.my/content/dam/hlb/my/docs/pdf/Personal/hl-connect/scan-qr/hlb-scan-qr-faq-en.pdf (Q3)
  - HLB: "When I want to request a payment from a few of my friends or multiple parties, can I share the same DuitNow QR Code to all of them? Yes, you can share the same DuitNow QR Code to them." Same PDF, Q17.
  - RHB: "Can I save and share the DuitNow QR code that I generated? Yes, you can by selecting 'Share QR' then save or share the QR image with others to receive money." https://www.rhbgroup.com/others/faq/payments-and-transfer/duitnow-qr/index.html
- The spec keeps the recipient ID stable: QR ID `26/02` is "Merchant/Recipient Identification Number assigned by Acquirer. For Dynamic QR – Merchant/Recipient ID should be maintained across different QR generated by the same Merchant/Recipient." https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/qr-data-object
- No published expiry for a static personal QR at any bank: **[U]**. No "reset my QR" function found on any reachable page: **[U]**.
- The owner can change the account behind it:
  - CIMB: "Tap 'QR Payment' > 'Default Account' > Select account for 'Transfer to Others', 'Payment to Merchants' or 'Receiving Money'". https://www.cimb.com.my/en/personal/help-support/faq/mobile-banking/cimb-octo-app/duitnow-qr/can-i-change-my-account-for-duitnow-qr-transactions.html
  - HLB: "To select the account to be used to receive payment, please tap on 'Receive QR Payment' > tap on the dropdown menu and select your preferred account". HLB PDF, Q8.
  - Whether the QR string changes when the account changes: **[U]**. [I] A saved code can go stale. Tapiro needs a "re-check your code" path.

### DuitNow QR with an amount (dynamic)

- HLB: "Tap on 'Generate QR with Amount' to get a Dynamic QR Code ... Please note that Dynamic QR Code has a 60-seconds validity." HLB PDF, Q3.
- HLB web page: "A Dynamic QR code, where you have set the amount to receive, cannot be shared out and has a 60 seconds validity". https://www.hlb.com.my/en/personal-banking/hong-leong-connect/qr-pay.html
- RHB: "the QR code with requested amount only valid for 60 seconds." RHB URL above.
- Spec: point of initiation `01` is "11 – Static QR Code 12 – Dynamic QR Code". PayNet data-object URL above.
- [I] Tapiro must reject a code with `010212` at setup. It will be dead within a minute.

### DuitNow ID (phone number)

- One ID, one account, and the owner can move it: "Each ID can only be linked to one bank account." "You can do so via your preferred bank's Internet or Mobile Banking, where you will be able to view, modify or transfer all your registered mobile number". https://paynet.my/resources/faq.json
- TNG drops the ID in two cases:
  - "If you have changed your mobile number in TNG eWallet app, your DuitNow ID will automatically be deregistered". https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/4405086048025
  - "Your DuitNow ID will be deregistered automatically upon termination." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/4405092951449
- [I] A DuitNow ID survives a change of bank. A saved QR may not. The ID is the more durable record.

### TNG personal receive QR

- TNG confirms a personal QR exists and is used for selling: "P2P seller is an existing Touch 'n Go eWallet user that is currently using their personal QR to receive payments for their goods or services sold to customers." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17608981927577
- TNG is a DuitNow acquirer: "TNG Digital Sdn Bhd | 890053 | TNGDMYNB". PayNet data-object URL above (Table 9).
- Expiry or reset of the TNG personal QR: **[U]**. No help article found by the Zendesk search API for "QR expire", "my QR code" or "save QR".

### Alipay 收钱码 and WeChat 收款码 (personal receive codes)

- Expiry, rotation and reset for personal receive codes: **[U]**. No consumer help page was reachable.
- What is documented is the merchant side and the paying side:
  - Alipay merchant order code: "当前预下单请求生成的二维码码串，有效时间2小时". Example "https://qr.alipay.com/bavh4wjlxf12tper3a". https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/05osv9.md
  - WeChat Pay merchant Native code: "code_url有效期为2小时，失效后需要重新请求该接口以获取新的code_url". Example "weixin://wxpay/bizpayurl/up?pr=NwY5Mz9&groupid=00". https://pay.weixin.qq.com/doc/v3/merchant/4012791877.md
- [I] Those are per-order merchant codes, not personal codes. They show that per-order codes expire. They say nothing about a personal static code.

### Paying codes (付款码 / consumer-presented) must never be shared

- Alipay: "付款码 ... 1分钟自动刷新一次；付款码用过一次后，无论成功、失败都不能再次使用。" https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/00f7o1.md
- Alipay format: "买家的付款码（25~30开头的长度为16~24位的数字...）". https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/02ekfp.md
- WeChat format: "用户付款码规则：18位纯数字，前缀以10-15开头的为付款码码段。" Path: "微信->"我"->"支付"->"收付款"". https://pay.weixin.qq.com/wiki/doc/api/micropay.php?chapter=5_1
- DuitNow consumer-presented QR is scanned by the merchant to debit the customer: "Customer logs into Online Banking or Mobile Banking app and initiates a QR code generation ... Merchant scans the QR code presented by the Customer ... Merchant initiates QR Debit request". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/consumer-presented-qr/consumer-presented-mode-domestic-qr
- [I] The receive code and the pay code sit on the same "收付款" screen in WeChat. A helper can pick the wrong one. Tapiro must detect and block pay codes.

## 2. Rules on personal collection codes

### PRC side (Alipay, WeChat)

- PBC rules on personal codes used for business (often cited as 银发〔2021〕259号, effective 2022-03-01, later softened): **[U]**. pbc.gov.cn and gov.cn were reachable, but I could not locate the notice or the follow-up statement. Do not cite dates until a lawyer or a fetched PBC page confirms them.
- Primary evidence that the split exists: Alipay's changelog: "因平台业务调整，当面付收款码相关能力请使用【经营码】。" https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/074mkv.md
- Alipay merchant collection needs a real business. "申请开通当面付是必须具备门店条件". "门店无禁限售相关内容，需要与营业执照经营范围相匹配。" https://ideservice.alipay.com/cms/site/0izalr
- Alipay or WeChat terms on using a personal code to sell services: **[U]**. P2P report §5 notes the WeChat Pay acceptable-use rule against "Academic fraud, essay ghostwriting, proxy test taking".
- Receiving from a foreigner, or from someone outside mainland China, into a personal code: **[U]**.
- [I] Both parties pay in CNY between mainland accounts for a service done in Malaysia. That raises PRC foreign-exchange and Malaysian exchange-control questions (P2P report §5). Tapiro cannot check any of this.

### Malaysian side (DuitNow, TNG, banks)

- DuitNow P2P QR is a standard use. MCC `52`: "'0000' to be used for P2P QRs". PayNet data-object URL above.
- TNG accepts personal QRs for small selling, and offers an upgrade:
  - "Can any user opt-in as a P2P seller? No. Only existing Malaysian Touch 'n Go eWallet user with an eKYC (Verified) account." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17608831653785
  - P2P Seller "Monthly Transaction Limit ... 20K *Premium tier only"; SME Merchant "No Limit". https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17607709095321
  - "Is there an opt-in fee and is it reversible? No, It is free and it is not reversible." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17608810959513
- TNG P2P needs a verified sender: "You have completed account verification (eKYC) as a Sender". https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/4403827914393
- TNG non-transferable balance cannot pay a P2P QR: it covers all uses "except Peer-to-Peer money transfer via QR Payment". https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/4847565113113
- TNG cannot pay itself: "self-transfer with your own QR code is not permitted." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/17607681874713
- Foreigners and DuitNow ID on TNG: "DuitNow Transfer is open for all users. However, only Malaysian mobile numbers can be registered as a DuitNow ID." https://support.tngdigital.com.my/hc/en-my/articles/4405086018329
- TNG or bank terms that forbid or limit personal-QR receipts from other people: **[U]**. TNG terms page returned "Page not found".

## 3. Fast setup on iOS

### Pick a screenshot and decode on device: yes, no Photos permission needed

- PHPicker runs out of process: "Because the system manages its life cycle in a separate process, it's private by default. The user doesn't need to explicitly authorize your app to select photos". https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/delivering-an-enhanced-privacy-experience-in-your-photos-app
- Filter to screenshots: `PHPickerFilter.screenshots`, "A filter that represents screenshots." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photosui/phpickerfilter-swift.struct
- Decode with Vision:
  - `DetectBarcodesRequest` (iOS 18.0+): "This request generates a collection of BarcodeObservation objects that describe each barcode the request detects." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/detectbarcodesrequest
  - `VNDetectBarcodesRequest` (iOS 11.0+, not deprecated): "A request that detects barcodes in an image." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vndetectbarcodesrequest
  - Payload: `payloadStringValue`, "A string value that represents the barcode payload ... a string representation of the payload may not be available." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vnbarcodeobservation/payloadstringvalue
  - Older path: `CIDetectorTypeQRCode`, "A detector that searches for Quick Response codes ... in a still image". https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreimage/cidetectortypeqrcode
- Expo path with no Swift:
  - `expo-image-picker`: "No permissions request is necessary for launching the image library." https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/imagepicker/
  - `expo-camera` `Camera.scanFromURLAsync(url, barcodeTypes)`: "Scan bar codes from the image at the given URL." "Note: Only QR codes are supported on iOS." https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/camera/
  - The image-picker config plugin writes `photosPermission` to "the `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` permission message". Same image-picker URL. [I] The key is present but no prompt shows for a library pick of a still image.
- Live camera scan is the fallback when the code is on another device. It needs camera permission: "Camera access always requires the user's permission." Image-picker URL above.

### Tell the network from the string

DuitNow (fully specified by PayNet, EMV-based):

- "The DuitNow National QR Code Standard is referenced to ... EMV® QR Code Specification for Payment Systems (EMV QRCPS) Merchant-Presented Mode". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/qr-generation-specification/merchant-presented-mode
- Tags, from the PayNet data-object URL:
  - `00` Payload Format Indicator: "Version '02'".
  - `26/00` AID: "Malaysia AID: A0000006150001".
  - `26/01` Acquirer ID, from Table 9. Examples: Maybank `588734`, CIMB `501854`, Public Bank `564162`, RHB `564160`, HLB `588830`, TNG `890053`, Boost `890061`, GrabPay `890046`, ShopeePay `890004`.
  - `52` MCC: "'0000' to be used for P2P QRs".
  - `53` currency: "Use 458 for Malaysian Ringgit." `58`: "Use 'MY' for Malaysia."
  - `59` Merchant Name: mandatory, "Var. up to 25". `60` Merchant City: mandatory.
  - `63` CRC: "[ISO/IEC 13239] using the polynomial '1021' (hex) and initial value 'FFFF' (hex)".
- PayNet's P2P example: `00020201021126410014A000000615000101065016640209123456789520400005303458540510.005802MY5909AUSERNAME6005BANGI63043A23`. https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/merchant-presented-qr/examples
  - I recomputed the CRC of both PayNet examples with polynomial 0x1021 and init 0xFFFF. Both match (`3A23`, `343F`). So a CRC check in the app is cheap and correct.
- [I] Validation rule: CRC valid, tag `26/00` = `A0000006150001`, `01` = `11`, `53` = `458`, `58` = `MY`. Then `52` = `0000` means a personal P2P code; any other MCC means a merchant code. Read `26/01` to label the bank or wallet.

Alipay and WeChat:

- Merchant order codes use `https://qr.alipay.com/...` and `weixin://wxpay/bizpayurl...` (quotes in §1).
- Personal receive code formats: **[U]**. Neither company documents them. [I] Matching on host `qr.alipay.com` or on a `wxp://` prefix is a heuristic, not a check. Keep it as a soft label only.

TNG: a TNG personal receive code that is a DuitNow QR will carry Acquirer ID `890053` [I]. Any TNG-only (non-DuitNow) format: **[U]**.

### Block paying codes

- [I] All three paying formats differ from receive codes:
  - Alipay 付款码: digits only, 16–24 long, starts 25–30 (quote in §1).
  - WeChat 付款码: digits only, 18 long, starts 10–15 (quote in §1).
  - DuitNow consumer-presented QR: EMV consumer-presented mode. Its exact encoding: **[U]** (EMVCo PDF not downloadable here). [I] It will not carry the merchant-presented tags above, so it fails the DuitNow MPM check.
- Rule: accept only (a) a valid DuitNow MPM string with `01` = `11`, or (b) a URL-form string for Alipay or WeChat. Reject all-digit strings with a message: "This is a payment code. It refreshes and lets anyone charge you. Use your receive code."

## 4. Share from the wallet app into Tapiro

- Bank apps share the receive QR as an image through a share action:
  - HLB: "Tap on 'Share My QR' > Tap on 'Share' > Select how you wish to share your QR Code to the payer." HLB PDF, Q3.
  - RHB: "selecting 'Share QR' then save or share the QR image". RHB URL above.
  - HLB web banking can "download it in .pdf format to receive payments." HLB PDF, Q15.
- Alipay, WeChat and TNG "save / share my receive code" help pages: **[U]**.
- To appear in that share sheet, Tapiro needs a Share extension. Apple: "Share extensions give users a convenient way to share content with other entities". https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/General/Conceptual/ExtensibilityPG/Share.html
  - Activation for images: `NSExtensionActivationSupportsImageWithMaxCount`, "The maximum number of image files that the app extension supports." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsextension/nsextensionattributes/nsextensionactivationrule/nsextensionactivationsupportsimagewithmaxcount
  - [I] An extension is a separate target. In this repo it needs a config plugin, because `ios/` is generated. It does not fit "one module facade" in `modules/tapiro-kit` cleanly. Defer it. "Save image" in the wallet, then pick it in Tapiro, gives the same result with no extension.
  - A PDF from HLB web banking will not go through the image picker. [I] Ask the helper to screenshot it.

## 5. DuitNow phone number: can Tapiro verify it

- No. Lookup is a network message between participants. Pay by Proxy "requires initiating a Proxy Resolution Enquiry request ... to retrieve the recipient's name for confirmation". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/transfer/integration/pay-by-proxy
- Participants check "Proxy is registered with a valid account number". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/validations
- Joining needs a licence (RPP admission list). https://paynet.my/resources/faq.json
- What is left:
  - The payer's bank shows the registered name. CIMB: "the recipient's name associated with the DuitNow ID will be displayed, please verify recipient's name prior to confirm transaction." (P2P report §4)
  - [I] Self-test: the helper sends RM1 to their own number from another account, or asks a friend. Tapiro can suggest it but cannot see the result.
  - [I] Format check only: a Malaysian mobile number. TNG accepts "only Malaysian mobile numbers" as DuitNow ID (quote in §2).
  - [I] Name match: ask the helper for "the name your bank shows". Show it to the poster with "Your bank should show this name." A mismatch is the poster's cue to stop.
- Bank account number: PayNet's recommended confirmation fields include "Beneficiary account name". https://docs.paynet.my/docs/duitnow-qr/validations. Tapiro cannot check the number. Per-bank account-number formats: **[U]**.

## 6. Privacy

- A DuitNow QR string contains personal data:
  - `59` Merchant Name is mandatory; PayNet's P2P sample is "5909AUSERNAME". [I] For a personal QR this is the helper's name as the bank holds it.
  - `26/04` "Merchant mobile number" is optional. `60` city is mandatory. `92` "Latitude and Longitude of the Merchant" is optional.
  - `26/02` QR ID is a stable recipient identifier.
  - Sources: PayNet data-object and examples URLs above.
- [I] The image adds nothing the string lacks, except screenshot clutter (status bar, balance, other UI). Store the decoded string, not the image. Render the QR from the unchanged string when the poster needs it. Never edit the string (P2P report §8 on MSBA risk).
- PDPA principles (Act 709): "take steps to ensure that the data is secure, not modified, misused or given to unauthorized parties" and "personal data is not allowed to be stored in a processing for more than the necessary time limit". https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/principles-of-personal-data-protection/
- [I] Consequences:
  - Show payee details only to the poster of a task the helper accepted, and only until the task closes plus a dispute window. Enforce with RLS, not UI.
  - Do not log the string. Do not send it to AI.
  - Show the helper what the code reveals before saving: "This code shows your bank name: AUSERNAME".
  - Delete with the account.
  - Data stays in Singapore Supabase; the cross-border assessment in `2026-10-04-backend-platform.md` §5 covers it.

## Recommended setup flow

Screens are native sheets pushed from Profile → "How you get paid". Every string is localised in zh-Hans, en and ms.

1. **List screen.** Methods the helper has added, each with a "Default" mark. Empty state: "Add how posters can pay you." Add button shows a native menu: DuitNow phone number, Bank account, DuitNow / TNG QR code, Cash, and (if decided, see below) Alipay or WeChat code.
2. **DuitNow phone number.** One phone field (`textContentType` telephone). Prefill from the account phone if it is `+60`. Second field: "Name your bank shows" (required). Footer: "Posters' banks will show this name. If it differs, they will stop." Optional link: "Test it: send RM1 to yourself from another account."
3. **Bank account.** Native picker for the bank, digits-only account field, the same name field. Mask the number after saving (last 4 digits).
4. **QR code.** Two buttons:
   - "Choose screenshot" (default). PHPicker filtered to screenshots, single selection. **No permission prompt.**
   - "Scan with camera" (fallback). Camera permission prompt only here, with a purpose string.
   - Decode on device. Never upload the image.
   - Validation and messages:
     - No QR found → "No QR code found. Crop closer and try again."
     - Payment code (digits-only Alipay/WeChat pattern) → block with the warning in §3.
     - DuitNow, CRC bad → "This code is damaged. Save it again from your bank app."
     - DuitNow, `01` = `12` → "This code has an amount and expires in 60 seconds. Use your static receive code."
     - DuitNow, MCC not `0000` → allow, label "Business QR".
     - Valid → confirm screen: bank or wallet name from Acquirer ID, and the name in tag `59`. "Posters will see: AUSERNAME, Maybank. Save?"
   - The helper's in-app instructions per wallet: "Open your bank app → Receive / Share QR → Save image" (copy confirmed for HLB and RHB only; others to be checked on device).
5. **Cash.** A toggle. No data.
6. **Re-check prompt.** Every 90 days, and whenever a poster reports "couldn't pay", ask the helper to re-pick the code. [I] Covers changed accounts, since no expiry is published.

Permissions: none for the default path. Camera only on the scan fallback. Do not add Photos library read access.

What to store, per method, in one `payee_methods` table:

- `type` (`duitnow_id`, `bank_account`, `duitnow_qr`, `alipay`, `wechat`, `cash`), `is_default`, `created_at`, `checked_at`.
- `duitnow_id`: phone number in E.164, `display_name`.
- `bank_account`: bank code (Table 9 Acquirer ID or BIC), account number, `display_name`.
- `duitnow_qr`: the raw string, plus parsed `acquirer_id`, `mcc`, `name` (tag `59`). No image.
- RLS: owner reads and writes; a poster reads only for a task where this helper is the accepted helper and the task is open or within the dispute window.

## Decisions for the human

- **Alipay / WeChat codes.**
  - A. Leave them out of v1 (recommended). Rules and cross-border status are **[U]**, and DuitNow covers students with Malaysian accounts.
  - B. Allow them as "Other code" with a weak label and a notice that Tapiro does not check them.
  - C. Allow them only after a lawyer confirms the PBC and exchange-control position.
- **Share extension.**
  - A. Defer (recommended). Save-then-pick works with no new target.
  - B. Build it now through a config plugin, outside the kit facade.
- **Decoder.**
  - A. `expo-camera` `scanFromURLAsync` plus `expo-image-picker` (recommended for speed). No Swift.
  - B. Vision `DetectBarcodesRequest` in `tapiro-kit`. Better control (screenshot filter, multiple codes in one image).
