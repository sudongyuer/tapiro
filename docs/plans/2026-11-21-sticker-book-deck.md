# Sticker book deck, calendar sheet and posters

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-21-sticker-book-deck-design.md`. Owner request (2026-11-21): 「参考视频优化我们的卡片和日历的
UI/UX，做的很类似都行，我喜欢这种UI/UX交互，然后分享的卡片的功能」, with a 16.7 s screen recording of a daily-card app. The
owner delegated the design (2026-11-17); every choice below is ours and listed for veto.

## What the reference does

- Deck: one white rounded card centred on a light page, two or three tilted cards behind it; the top card follows a
  horizontal swipe with a slight tilt and leaves to the side while the next card rises from the stack. Date pill at the
  top centre, 「Today」 on the right; two bottom pills, a light one and a black 「Share」.
- Calendar: a large sheet titled 「Calendar」 with ×; months scroll continuously; a day with an entry shows the
  illustration instead of the number; weekday initials; a floating black 「Share September」 pill naming the month in view.
- Share: a sheet with a poster (the month on a white rounded panel over a colourful collage, wordmark at the bottom,
  small round style buttons at the top right) and a bottom row: share icon, 「Save」, 「Story」.
