## Findings

- The dark stamp bug (dark success green on the white receipt, 2.0:1) was pinned by a unit test
  (`pins.test.ts` expected `#3ECF8E`) and by three UI cases that looked for `--color-success` in the current appearance:
  the checks asserted the colour that was wrong, not that the stamp could be read. The stamp tests now assert paper
  green on white, and `tokens.test.ts` asserts the pair's contrast, so a stamp ink that cannot be read on the receipt
  fails there first.
- `tokens.test.ts` already covered each status colour on white and on the dark surface, but not on the tinted surfaces
  where they are also drawn (the peach ride ticket). The new ticket inks have their own test on both ticket surfaces.
- Same-pattern sweep: every `--color-success` / `--color-urgent` / `--color-warning` use on a tinted or paper surface
  was listed (`LiveCard`, `SettledCard`, `appliedStamp`, `pins.ts`, `RideTicket`, `FullTicket`, `ApplicationCard`);
  the remaining ones sit on system surfaces or pick their tone from the surface's luminance.
