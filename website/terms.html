The service

Tapiro is operated by Su Dongyu, an individual. Tapiro lets students post everyday errands and shared rides and find someone nearby to help. Tapiro connects users; it is not a party to the arrangements between them.

Payments between users

Rewards and shared fares are paid directly between users through their own bank or e-wallet apps, or in cash. Tapiro does not hold, transfer or guarantee users' money. Both sides confirm in the app when a payment is made and received.

Tapiro Coins

Coins are a digital item used only inside Tapiro for app features such as extra posts and AI drafting. Coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred, exchanged, cashed out or used to pay other users. Free coins do not expire.

Buying coins

Coins can be bought on tapiro.app by card, processed securely by Stripe. Prices are shown in RM and include all fees. Coins are added to your Tapiro account immediately after payment and appear in the app the next time it refreshes.

Refunds and cancellations

If a post you paid for with coins is cancelled before anyone accepts it, or nobody accepts it, the coin is returned to your balance automatically.

For coin purchases, contact us at sudongyu3@gmail.com within 14 days if a purchase was made in error or the coins were not delivered, and we will refund unused coins in full to the original payment method within 10 business days. Purchases made through the App Store are refunded through Apple.

No illegal, dangerous or prohibited items or tasks, no harassment, fraud or impersonation, and no academic dishonesty. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

Liability

Users are responsible for the errands they post and accept. To the extent permitted by law, Tapiro is not liable for losses arising from arrangements between users.
