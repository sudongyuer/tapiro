The service

Tapiro is operated by Su Dongyu, an individual. Tapiro lets students post everyday errands and shared rides and find someone nearby to help. Tapiro connects users; it is not a party to the arrangements between them.

Payments between users

Rewards and shared fares are paid directly between users through their own bank or e-wallet apps, or in cash. Tapiro does not hold, transfer or guarantee users' money. Both sides confirm in the app when a payment is made and received.

Tapiro Coins

Coins are a digital item used only inside Tapiro for app features such as extra posts, or swapped for a leaf that refills Ask Tapir's energy. Coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred, exchanged, cashed out or used to pay other users. Free coins do not expire.

Buying coins

Coins are bought inside the Tapiro app through Apple’s in-app purchase, and Apple handles the payment. Prices are App Store prices shown in RM in the app. Coins are added to your Tapiro account as soon as our server verifies the purchase with Apple; while it is being verified, the app shows the purchase as processing. Purchased coins do not expire.

Refunds and cancellations

If a post you paid for with coins is cancelled before anyone accepts it, or nobody accepts it, the coin is returned to your balance automatically.

Coin purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com under Apple’s terms. If Apple refunds a purchase, the refunded coins are removed from your balance. If coins you paid for did not arrive, contact us at sudongyu3@gmail.com and we will add them once we confirm the purchase.

Ask Tapir

Ask Tapir is optional and works only after you agree on its consent screen; the Privacy Policy says what is sent to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Singapore). Tapir drafts bounties, answers questions, translates messages you pick and writes down what you say when your phone cannot. It never posts or sends anything for you: you check a draft and post it yourself, and you can always fill things in without Tapir. Its energy refills every morning and unused energy is not kept; leaves never expire.

Acceptable use

No illegal, dangerous or prohibited items or tasks, no harassment, fraud or impersonation, and no academic dishonesty. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

Liability

Users are responsible for the errands they post and accept. To the extent permitted by law, Tapiro is not liable for losses arising from arrangements between users.
