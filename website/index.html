Help each other, nearby.

Tapiro is an iOS app for students living abroad. Post a small errand — picking up a parcel, buying food, queueing, sharing a ride home — and a fellow student nearby can help.

What you can do

Everyday help between students, built for iPhone.

Bounties

Post an errand with a reward you set. A nearby student applies, you choose who helps, and both sides confirm when it's done.

Carpool

Going the same way after class? Start a shared Grab ride and split the fare with classmates heading home.

Trust and reviews

Reliability levels, two-sided reviews and stickers earned by helping, so you know who you're dealing with.

Three languages

Simplified Chinese, English and Bahasa Melayu.
