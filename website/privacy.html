Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What we collect

Your account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google, the display name and avatar you choose, the errands and rides you post or join, messages, reviews, and the payee details you choose to add (for example a DuitNow phone number or a payment QR code). With your permission we use your approximate location to show errands nearby.

We do not collect identity card, passport or student ID numbers, and we never collect card details. Card payments on our website are processed by Stripe, which receives your card details directly.

How we use it

To run the app: matching errands with helpers nearby, showing a payee's details only to the person paying them, reminders and notifications, reliability levels, and preventing fraud and abuse.

AI features (Ask Tapir)

Ask Tapir is optional. It uses Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Singapore) through our own server, and only after you agree on its consent screen. You can turn it off at any time in the app under Me → Tapir; while it is off, Tapir does not use the cloud and you fill things in yourself.

What is sent: what you type or say and the pictures you pick, to draft bounties and answer questions; a chat message you choose to translate; your recording, when you talk to Tapir and your phone cannot recognise the speech itself; and the places and habits you let Tapir remember (Tapir Remembers), with drafts and answers only.

What is not sent: your payee details, and messages or data you did not pick.

What is kept: conversations and drafts stay on your phone. Our server keeps only your consent record and usage, never the conversation, plus the notes in Tapir Remembers. Data sent to Model Studio is not used to train models.

Tapir Remembers: Tapir asks before it remembers a place or habit, and you can add notes yourself. In Me → Tapir → Tapir Remembers you can view, change or delete each note, delete them all, or turn it off; while it is off, Tapir remembers nothing new and does not use your notes. Deleting your account deletes them too.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Sign-in credentials are kept in your device's Keychain.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with the service providers above (including Stripe for website payments), and when required by law.

Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, or delete your account. Contact us at sudongyu3@gmail.com.
