Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What we collect

Your account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google, the display name and avatar you choose, the errands and rides you post or join, messages, reviews, and the payee details you choose to add (for example a DuitNow phone number or a payment QR code). With your permission we use your approximate location to show errands nearby.

We do not collect identity card, passport or student ID numbers, and we never collect card details. Card payments on our website are processed by Stripe, which receives your card details directly.

How we use it

To run the app: matching errands with helpers nearby, showing a payee's details only to the person paying them, reminders and notifications, reliability levels, and preventing fraud and abuse.

AI features

Optional AI drafting is processed by Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Singapore region) through our own server. We ask for your consent before the first use. Data sent for AI drafting is not used to train models.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Sign-in credentials are kept in your device's Keychain.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with the service providers above (including Stripe for website payments), and when required by law.

Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, or delete your account. Contact us at sudongyu3@gmail.com.
